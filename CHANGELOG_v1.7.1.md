# Mastervolt Energy v1.7.1

## History charts

- The time-axis labels now follow the selected Zoom (hrs): time for short ranges, date and time for intermediate ranges, weekday/date for multi-day ranges, and date/year for long ranges.
- Charging current and DC load current are now stacked bar charts, consistent with the power and storage charts.
- Chart legends are interactive. Selecting a component isolates it; selecting Total restores the complete stacked chart and total line.
- The selected legend item is visually highlighted.
