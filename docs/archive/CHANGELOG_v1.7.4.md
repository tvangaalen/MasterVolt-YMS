# Mastervolt Energy v1.7.4

## Battery cell-voltage charts

- Total view now draws genuinely cumulative smooth lines at each measurement: Cell 1, Cell 1+2, Cell 1+2+3, and Cell 1+2+3+4.
- Selecting Cell 1–4 shows only the raw, non-cumulative voltage of that individual cell.
- The per-measurement four-cell average voltage and BMS Max difference remain visible in both Total and individual-cell views.
